🎵 Nursery Rhyme Video Maker

Type or pick a rhyme. The tool sings it with a free AI voice and a music-box tune, animates cartoon scenes and karaoke lyrics, and renders a finished video.

Write your own original rhyme, or edit the classic. Words like star, moon, sheep, boat, rain, duck, cow, apple and flower add matching cartoon characters.
Ready. Click “Create voice & scenes”. The first run downloads a free voice model (about 90 MB).
Honest note: this makes animated cartoon-style videos (emoji characters, moving scenes, karaoke lyrics) with a free AI voice. It is not generative AI video, which needs paid or very heavy servers. The built-in rhymes are traditional public-domain verses. If you post to YouTube, mark kids’ videos as “Made for kids”.
