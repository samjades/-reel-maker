🎵 Nursery Rhyme Video Maker

Type or pick a rhyme. The tool paints a free AI cartoon picture for every line, adds slow camera motion, a singing AI voice, a music-box tune and karaoke lyrics, then renders a finished video.

Write your own original rhyme, or edit the classic. Words like star, moon, sheep, boat, rain, duck, cow, apple and flower add matching cartoon characters.
🎹 Music studio
Choose “Auto-compose from my lyrics” as the melody and the tool writes a tune that follows your words: one note per syllable, repeated lines get the same tune, and rhyming lines answer each other. Changed the music settings? Use “Rebuild music only” — no need to redo the voice or pictures.
🎥 Background video footage

Real footage mode: the tool searches Pexels (free, commercial use allowed) for clips of children, families, teachers and your lyric topics, plays them behind your song, and adds a beat-pulsing colour frame, bouncing stickers and karaoke lyrics. Pexels asks for credit, so copy the credits box into your video description. If clips cannot be loaded (browser blocking), upload your own clips instead.

AI pictures mode: each line gets an AI-painted cartoon picture from the free Pollinations service (your lyrics are sent to it as the picture description). The pictures are then animated with zoom, pan, fades and sparkles. True moving AI clips (like Sora or Veo) are not available free without limits, so this storybook style is what free tools can reliably do. The free service is slow at busy times and may show a small logo on some pictures; if a picture fails, that line falls back to the emoji scene. Built-in rhymes are traditional public-domain verses. For YouTube, mark kids’ videos as “Made for kids”.
