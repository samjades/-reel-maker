RhymeMagic AI

Free · runs in your browser

Type or pick a rhyme. RhymeMagic puts your words on a happy beat, sings them with a bouncy melody, adds cartoon friends and a bouncing-ball lyric line, then saves a video for YouTube.

1Your rhyme

Paste any text and it is cut into singable lines automatically. Repeating words (like a chorus) works great.

2Voice & beat

122 BPM
Band: upbeat claps, light drums, acoustic guitar, maracas and tambourine, with a bell playing the tune. Made fresh in your browser every time.

3Look

Only MP4/WebM clips with a reusable Creative Commons or public-domain license are offered. Pick a preview to use it. Your selected clip is placed behind the cartoon friends and lyrics and credited in the video description.
Backgrounds are searched from Wikimedia Commons directly in your browser. Availability changes over time, so check the selected footage before publishing. Landscape footage is cropped to fill the frame. The drawn scene is used if no video loads.

4Make it

First time: a ~90 MB voice model downloads once (needs internet), then it is remembered. Composing takes about a minute for a short rhyme.
Pick a starter rhyme or type your own, then press “Compose the song”.

Post it to your YouTube channel

Your video has been saved. Edit the text, copy it, then open the upload page and choose the saved file.
