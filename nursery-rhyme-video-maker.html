🎵 Nursery Rhyme Video Maker

Type or pick a rhyme. The tool paints a free AI cartoon picture for every line, adds slow camera motion, a singing AI voice, a music-box tune and karaoke lyrics, then renders a finished video.

Write your own original rhyme, or edit the classic. Words like star, moon, sheep, boat, rain, duck, cow, apple and flower add matching cartoon characters.
Ready. Click “Create voice & scenes”. The first run downloads a free voice model (about 90 MB).
How “free AI video” works here: each line gets an AI-painted cartoon picture from the free Pollinations service (your lyrics are sent to it as the picture description). The pictures are then animated with zoom, pan, fades and sparkles. True moving AI clips (like Sora or Veo) are not available free without limits, so this storybook style is what free tools can reliably do. The free service is slow at busy times and may show a small logo on some pictures; if a picture fails, that line falls back to the emoji scene. Built-in rhymes are traditional public-domain verses. For YouTube, mark kids’ videos as “Made for kids”.
