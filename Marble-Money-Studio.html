MARBLE MONEY STUDIO

Pick a game

Four fun ways to learn about money. Tap one to start. Choose the screen shape first if you plan to record a video.

🪙

Budget Marbles

Your paycheck falls as marbles through the pegs into bins like rent, food and savings. Cha-ching when savings land.

💰

Savings Ladder

A gold coin hops up a 52-week saving challenge, adds each week’s amount and drops into the piggy bank.

📈

Growth Race

Three savers put away the same amount each month. Watch towers of money grow and see why starting early beats starting late.

🍞

Inflation Melt

Same money, but prices rise. Watch loaves of buying power vanish from cash, shrink in savings, or hold up when money grows.
